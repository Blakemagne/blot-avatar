run the combination and pose checks with `node --import tsx scripts/wardrobe-audit.mjs`. run `npm run site` followed by `node scripts/wardrobe-check.mjs` for the browser regressions. run `node scripts/wardrobe-power-check.mjs` for the powered material, turned eye and SVG fallback checks. the browser scripts accept `PLAYWRIGHT_CORE` and `CHROMIUM` for local installations. use `CHROMIUM_ANGLE=gl` to select hardware OpenGL for reference comparisons. check all 129 picker tiles against fresh renders with `node scripts/portrait-sheet.mjs --check`. the comparison checks the tile order and the colour and opacity of small regions within each tile. regenerate the sheet with `node scripts/portrait-sheet.mjs` using the same browser setup.

the [reference comparison](dot-parity.md) defines the 99.99% silhouette, exact foreground and exact component gates. all three must pass for each reference case. the wardrobe checks above measure transfer behavior. their counts do not measure pixel agreement.

## studio and headwear review

the size slider measures the longest visible axis of the resting body. on the default desktop stage, all 50 picker shapes and 116 preset buttons produced bare bodies spanning 180–181 pixels. on the 390-pixel mobile viewport they spanned 150–151 pixels. accessories retain their proportions. at the maximum slider setting, all 438 permitted shape and extra selections and all 116 dressed preset buttons fit inside both the 320-pixel and desktop stages. the paint brush’s headphone arch previously extended 45 pixels past the mobile stage. reproduce the size and clearance sweep with `node scripts/studio-framing.mjs` after building the site.

sunny and onyx now use a fitted cap with a continuous dome and a brim. across the 12,244 labelled pixels of sunny’s cap, mean absolute premultiplied channel error fell from 2.35 to 1.12 on a 0–255 scale. exact agreement rose from 8.36% to 31.00%. onyx’s cap improved from 1.82 to 1.08. the black beret’s main surface on the blue catalog flower improved from 5.12 to 4.42. its reference highlight is still sharper. [registered cap and beret close-ups](dot-review/caps-and-beret.webp) · [both turns and front view](dot-review/caps-and-beret-turns.webp)

the heart and capsule offer original, left and right positions for the beanie, hat and beret. imported shapes can define their own accessory and glasses lists and named horizontal positions. [shape definition and example](../site/prompt.md)

on moxie, classic sunglasses touched the canvas edge during low sweep, considering two options and disagreeing. the old bounds included hats but used only the body radius on wearers with glasses alone. replaying the same 1,102 motion frames at 144 px reduced clipped frames from 11 to zero. the motion regression includes moxie, both directions, normal and double strength, and the midpoint of every clip.

a second pass separated the curvature of the monocle cord from the ring and fitted the bevel of each tall oval rim. the fits use 48 landmarks per rim across 89 body and eye combinations. on the capsule's 2,580 labelled monocle pixels, mean channel error fell from 8.14 to 1.17 and exact agreement rose from 36.55% to 63.91%. all seven affected frontal outfits and their seven independent turned views improved. ## reference results

josh’s neutral reference mask covers 6,434 pixels on the tuft. on those pixels the mean absolute premultiplied channel error fell from 9.44 to 6.82 on a 0–255 scale. the pale lenses improved from 12.83 to 6.63, the dark pupils from 2.46 to 1.05, and the rims from 5.31 to 3.73. both builds ran through the same capture script. the tuft still has a visible boundary where it meets the body.
